9 District Tribal Handloom Traditions

Lamka Hub actively celebrates and preserves the unique weaving motifs and ceremonial drapes of all 9 indigenous tribes of Churachandpur District.

1

Kuki Traditional Weaves

Saipikhup quail-foot diamond shawl, Thangsuo honor drape & Pon Laili.

2

Paite Traditional Weaves

Puandum vertical stripe puan, Khamtang formal wear & Nikhap drapes.

3

Hmar Traditional Weaves

Thangsuo Puan, Khavawpuan honor drapes & Nzo ceremonial patterns.

4

Vaiphei Traditional Weaves

Khiangte pattern drapes, Suankhai motif & organic cotton loin weaves.

5

Zou Traditional Weaves

Zou Traditional Puan with geometric border weaves & vegetable dye yarn.

6

Gangte Traditional Weaves

Ancestral Gangte Puan, ceremonial wraps & backstrap loom textiles.

7

Simte Traditional Weaves

Handcrafted Simte Puan, traditional loin loom motifs & shawls.

8

Tedim Chin Weaves

Tedim Chin pattern drapes with gold accent thread & ceremonial shawls.

9

Kom Traditional Weaves

Kom Handloom creations, vibrant cultural wraps & loin loom drapes.
